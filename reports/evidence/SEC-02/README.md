## Retention / restriction policy as exercised

Raw evidence retention per class (default 90 days, owner-editable 1–3650) governs content reads (`expired`); typed journal entries remain queryable and exportable with their content hash. Owner restriction appends an immutable `evidence_redactions` row and removes the live file; projections (Rust engine) never hold raw evidence; backups taken afterwards carry the ledger row (documented in docs/operations.md). Exports list restricted/redacted/expired objects by status only. Every export, restriction and retention change is a `security_audit` row.

## Not covered

- The byte-cap truncation path was exercised through the builder (2,500 B cap) in the integration test, not through HTTP on a >64 MiB workspace.
- Agents can still read evidence metadata through the ordinary journal routes (by design, read-only grant); they cannot export bundles.
